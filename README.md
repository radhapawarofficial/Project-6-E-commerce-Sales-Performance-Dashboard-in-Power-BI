# 🚀Project 6 : E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

An advanced, comprehensive Business Intelligence solution built in **Microsoft Power BI** designed to track, analyze, and visualize retail e-commerce transactions, profitability margins, customer behaviors, and multi-channel payment trends in real time.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This dashboard transforms complex, multi-regional e-commerce transaction data into clear, actionable insights. Stakeholders and analysts can monitor overarching business health or drill down into specific regional performances, product categories, and transaction trends across the year.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

- **Executive KPI Summary Cards**: Instant visibility into critical business metrics:
  - **Total Sales**: 438K
  - **Total Profit**: 113K
  - **Total Quantity**: 5,615 units
  - **Total Orders**: 500 orders
  - **Total Customers**: 336 unique customers
- **Interactive Global Slicers**: Real-time multi-level filtering by **State**, **City**, **Sub-Category**, **Category**, and **Payment Mode**.
- **Dynamic Visualizations**:
  - **Total Sales by State**: Regional revenue distribution highlighting top-performing states like Maharashtra (102K) and Madhya Pradesh (87K).
  - **Profit & Sales by Month**: Dual-metric tracking of profitability trends alongside gross sales month-over-month.
  - **Total Sales by Payment Mode**: Proportional analysis covering COD, Credit Card, UPI, EMI, and Debit Card transactions.
  - **Total Sales by Category**: Doughnut chart breaking down performance across major verticals (*Furniture*, *Clothing*, and *Electronics*).
  - **Total Sales by Sub-Category**: Ranked bar chart highlighting top-selling items such as Printers, Sarees, Bookcases, and Phones.
  - **Total Quantity by Month**: Trend area chart illustrating seasonal volume shifts throughout the year.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

- **Business Intelligence**: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling & DAX**: Custom Measures, Calculated Columns, and Relationships
- **Data Source**: E-Commerce Transaction & Sales Dataset
